Your data stays yours.

Nullen has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. This page explains exactly what that means.

What Nullen collects

Nothing. There is no sign-up, so there are no accounts. The page has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking scripts, and it does not set cookies or store anything in your browser.

Where your password is made

In your browser, on your device, using the system's secure random generator. The password is never sent to Nullen or anyone else, and it is not saved. When you close or refresh the page, it is gone.

What the host can see

Nullen is served through Cloudflare. Like any website, Cloudflare handles the technical request that loads the page, which includes your IP address and browser details, and it may keep security logs. That is about loading the page, not about passwords, which never leave your device.

Fonts and third parties

The page loads its fonts from nullen.cc itself and uses no third-party scripts, so your visit does not reach any other company beyond the host.

Questions about privacy? Email hello@nullen.cc. Last updated October 12, 2026.
